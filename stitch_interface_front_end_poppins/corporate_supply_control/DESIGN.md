## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a **Corporate Modern with High-Contrast Precision** aesthetic, tailored for enterprise operational control, asset tracking, and executive financial auditing. It blends rigorous administrative reliability with swift, tactical readability.

### Brand Personality & Core Mood
- **Unflinching Precision & Integrity:** Designed for warehouse managers, operations clerks, and financial controllers who cannot tolerate ambiguity. The UI radiates zero-loss accountability, clarity, and governance.
- **Alert & Dynamic:** Critical expiry alerts, threshold dropouts, and financial leakages command immediate visual prominence without creating visual noise or panicky interfaces.
- **Executive Elegance:** High-level metrics, loss comparisons, and departmental ranks are rendered with clean data hierarchies, subtle architectural framing, and authoritative corporate presence.

### Target Personas & Use Environment
- **Almoxarifado & Floor Operations:** High-speed data entry, barcode-ready scanning inputs, explicit status badges, and large touch targets for warehouse tablets.
- **Department Managers:** Agile requisition workflows, quick-glance budget quotas, and effortless approvals.
- **C-Level & Financial Auditing:** High-density summaries, loss-prevention scorecards, and audit logs delivered with crystal-clear numbers and unambiguous trend markers.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around strong institutional contrast, anchored by **Deep Royal Navy (`#1F0062`)** for authoritative structural framing and critical navigation, counterbalanced by **Electric Active Blue (`#0B78B3`)** for interactive affordances, active selections, and progressive actions.

### Palette Hierarchy & Intent
- **Primary (`#1F0062`):** Defines headers, primary buttons, corporate branding anchors, sidebar structures, and major KPI labels.
- **Secondary (`#0B78B3`):** Guides user interaction. Used for active tabs, links, primary interactive states, focus rings, and transactional progress.
- **Alert & Critical Accent (`#D73220`):** Strictly reserved for urgent actions: expired supplies, high financial discrepancies, out-of-stock indicators, and destructive deletion confirmations.
- **Burgundy Accent (`#68150A`):** Used for deep financial audits, severe loss penalties, and high-impact threshold warnings.
- **Surface Warm Neutral (`#F9ECE5`):** Serves as an intentional, warm tinted highlight surface for notification panels, requisition callouts, and badge backgrounds, preventing cold corporate sterility.
- **Neutral Backgrounds (`#FFFFFF`, `#F8FAFC`, `#1E293B`):** Crisp white for foreground data cards and operational tables; slate canvas for structural contrast; high-density slate-900 for dark typography and data headers.

## Typography

The typography is driven entirely by **Poppins**, delivering geometric clarity, open counters, and high immediate legibility under rapid data entry conditions. For batch codes, tracking numbers, and technical lot serials, **JetBrains Mono** is injected to avoid character confusion (such as `0` vs `O` and `1` vs `l`).

### Typographic Hierarchy Rules
- **Display & Financial Big Numbers:** Use `display-lg` with `fontWeight: 700` for monthly total losses and aggregate stock valuations.
- **Section Headers & Tables:** Use `title-lg` or `headline-sm` with semi-bold weighting (`600`) to anchor data partitions.
- **Table Data & Operative Text:** Render general inventories and descriptions using `body-md` (`14px/22px`) with regular weight.
- **Badges, Status Tags & Metadata:** Render all uppercase category identifiers, stock indicators, and unit tags using `label-sm` with letter spacing (`0.05em`) to guarantee quick peripheral scanning.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column fluid grid system paired with an 8pt architectural rhythm, tailored for data-dense dashboards, split-screen forms, and high-volume table displays.

### Layout Breakpoints
- **Desktop (`>= 1280px`):** Fixed 260px collapsible sidebar for administrative navigation, fluid 12-column master canvas with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`.
- **Tablet / Industrial Rugged Screen (`768px - 1279px`):** Top navigation with icon drawer, 8-column layout with `gutter: 1rem` and `margin: 1.25rem`. Optimized for inventory counts in physical warehouses.
- **Mobile (`< 768px`):** Single-column stacked workflow with floating bottom bar for instantaneous barcode and inventory entry, with `margin-mobile: 1rem`.

### Spacing Application
- **Component Padding:** `space-sm` for dense table rows and quick dropdown items; `space-md` for standard cards and form field insets; `space-lg` for modal shells and overview metric containers.
- **Section Rhythm:** Maintain `space-xl` (2rem) between distinct metric groups and inventory tables.

## Elevation & Depth

The design system utilizes **Tonal Stratification** paired with subtle, calibrated ambient drop-shadows. The layout keeps a flat, fast-loading posture ideal for GitHub Pages while ensuring clear visual separation between the canvas, operational cards, and alert layers.

### Elevation Levels
- **Canvas Base (Level 0):** Pure slate tint `#F8FAFC`, non-elevated.
- **Standard Card & Grid Panel (Level 1):** Solid white surface `#FFFFFF` with a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) and an ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(31, 0, 98, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(31, 0, 98, 0.03)`.
- **Active / Hover State (Level 2):** Elevated card or hovered inventory row: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(31, 0, 98, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(31, 0, 98, 0.05)`.
- **Floating Modals, Drawers & Action Bars (Level 3):** Higher visual priority: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(31, 0, 98, 0.12), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(31, 0, 98, 0.08)`.
- **Critical Expiration Flash Alerts:** Emphasized via a dual border technique—left inset thick border (`4px solid #D73220`) over warm background `#F9ECE5` rather than high z-index shadows.

## Shapes

The design system employs **Level 2 (Rounded - 0.5rem / 8px base)** for standard controls and panels, providing an ergonomic, contemporary feel while preserving the precise geometry needed for dense data layouts.

### Geometry Specifications
- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Buttons, Selects):** `0.5rem` (8px). Maintains a balanced touch boundary that feels solid and responsive.
- **Data Cards & Containers:** `0.75rem` (12px) to frame multi-column data views cleanly.
- **Badges, Counters & Status Tags:** Fully rounded pill (`9999px`) to immediately distinguish contextual states and batch tags from rectangular action buttons.
- **Modal Dialogs & Slide-overs:** `1rem` (16px) with smooth corner cutoffs for modern desktop appearance.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Background in Deep Royal Navy (`#1F0062`), text white, font weight `600`, padding `0.625rem 1.25rem`, radius `8px`. Hover: `filter: brightness(1.2)` with light navy shadow.
- **Secondary / Action Button:** Background Electric Blue (`#0B78B3`), text white, radius `8px`. Used for "Adicionar Lote" (Add Batch) or "Transferir Insumo" (Transfer Supply).
- **Destructive / Loss Button:** Background Alert Red (`#D73220`), text white. Triggers loss logs (`LOSS_EXPIRED`, `LOSS_MISSING`).
- **Ghost / Outlined Button:** Transparent background, 1.5px border `#E2E8F0`, text Navy (`#1F0062`). Hover: background `#F1F5F9`.

### 2. Status Badges & Chips
- **Em Dia (In Stock):** Background `#ECFDF5`, text `#065F46`, border `#A7F3D0`.
- **Atenção (< 30 Dias Vencimento):** Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#92400E`, border `#FDE68A`.
- **Vencido / Perda Crítica:** Background `#FEE2E2`, text `#991B1B`, border `#FECACA`. Left icon dot included.
- **Departamento Chip:** Background `#F9ECE5`, text `#68150A`, font weight `600`, radius `9999px`, padding `0.25rem 0.625rem`.

### 3. Inventory & Financial Data Tables
- **Header:** Background `#F8FAFC`, uppercase Poppins `11px`, letter-spacing `0.05em`, color `#64748B`, bottom border `2px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **Rows:** Alternating subtle row backgrounds (`#FFFFFF` to `#FBFDFF`), height `52px`, padding `0 1rem`. Critical warning rows highlight with a left `3px solid #D73220` bar.
- **Monetary Values & Batch Numbers:** Tabular numeric alignment, rendered with `JetBrains Mono` for rapid cross-checking.

### 4. Input Fields & Form Controls
- **Standard Inputs:** Height `44px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`, radius `8px`, padding `0 0.875rem`. Focus ring: `2px solid #0B78B3` with zero offset.
- **Barcode & Batch Scan Input:** Accentuated with an Electric Blue left-side icon, autofocus styling, and mono font for rapid serial entry.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Primary Navy fill (`#1F0062`) when checked with pure white checkmark indicator.

### 5. Audit & Metric Cards (Executive Dashboard)
- **Loss Prevention Metric Card:** White surface, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, top color border line (Navy for total volume, Alert Red for financial losses, Electric Blue for routine consumption).
- **Metric Content:** Upper label in `label-md` uppercase `#64748B`, metric in `display-lg` Poppins bold, footer with delta indicator (e.g., `-14% no descarte de insumos`).
